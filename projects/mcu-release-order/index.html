Every movie, series and special from Iron Man (2008) through VisionQuest (2026), in the order audiences actually got them. What’s in, what’s out

Dates are US premiere dates. Included: Marvel Studios films (including the Sony co-productions with Spider-Man), Marvel Studios and Marvel Television series on Disney+, the Special Presentations, the I Am Groot shorts, the Marvel One-Shots and the Team Thor shorts (listed where they sit in the story, with their later home-video release noted). MCU Adjacent, off by default: the Fox X-Men films leading up to Logan, Deadpool 1 and 2, the Raimi and Webb Spider-Man films, the Venom films, the Netflix Marvel series (split into season buckets only around The Defenders, where the seasons actually interleave), Inhumans, Cloak & Dagger, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Agent Carter and Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man. Not included: X-Men ’97, Dark Phoenix, The New Mutants, the Fox Fantastic Four films, Sony’s other Spider-Man Universe films, the other Marvel TV series (Runaways, Helstrom), Spider-Noir, and theatrical re-releases.

The green-glow titles are Disney+’s official “Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday” list, published in August 2026: 15 picks, with both seasons of Loki counted as one. X-Men and X2 are on it but are MCU Adjacent, so they appear when that switch is on, or when you use the Doomsday filter.

Connections list what each title sets up or pays off. They are limited to links that are confirmed in the stories themselves or stated by Marvel, and the pairs are written once and shown from both sides.
