## 2.4.1 — 2026-11-15

- The title always reads **Hunter Pet Training Guide**. The line under it names the family you're viewing and its skills.
- The version number moved from the corner to beside the title.
- The Theme button is now a **sun / moon** picker that shows which theme is on.
- Dark is now the default theme. A theme you've already picked is kept.

## 2.4.0 — 2026-11-15

- **All families** moved from the tab row into a **Browse all…** button next to the Pet family picker. It opens every family in a popup; pick one to switch to it.
- Unique abilities, skills only one family can learn (like Lightning Breath for Wind Serpents), are starred in the family list.
- Overview shows what's unique to the family you're viewing.

## 2.3.0 — 2026-11-15

- New **Global** continent option, now the default. It lists pets from both Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor together.
- New **Show every rank** box on the training plan. Each leg lists every rank in order, so you can learn a skill one rank at a time instead of skipping ranks.
- The version number in the corner opens this changelog.

## 2.2.0 — 2026-11-13

- Plan A and Plan B are now one training plan, split into 10-level legs (10–19, 20–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50–60).

## 2.1.0 — 2026-11-13

- New **Hunter level** box. Tick "Hide pets above my level" to hide pets you can't tame yet.
- Plans and the multi-skill pet list say when pets are hidden by your level rather than missing.
- Ticked skills and finished stops drop out of the plans. Tick "Show checked" to see them again.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-13

- Rebuilt the guide as a faster React app. Saved settings and ticks carry over.
- Pet and ability data is checked for mistakes before each release.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-13

- Added a site icon and a thumbnail.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-13

- First release: training plans, attack speeds, every rank and its pets, all families, and quirks for every hunter pet family.
